# Financial Statement Analysis

An Excel workbook that calculates common financial ratios from a two-year income statement and balance sheet, and shows how each ratio changed.

> **All data is fictional.** "Sample Retail Co." and its numbers were made up for practice. They are not from any real company.

## What's inside

| Tab | What it does |
|---|---|
| Statements | Income statement and balance sheet for FY2024 and FY2025 ($ in thousands), with subtotals and a balance check |
| Ratios | Margins, liquidity, leverage, return on equity, inventory turnover, and revenue growth, with year-over-year change |

## Ratios covered

Gross margin, operating margin, net profit margin, current ratio, quick ratio, debt-to-equity, return on equity, inventory turnover, revenue growth.

## What the sample shows

Revenue grew 15% from FY2024 to FY2025, all three margins improved, and debt-to-equity fell, meaning the company relied less on debt.

## Notes

- ROE and inventory turnover use year-end balances instead of averages, to keep the model simple.
- Income tax is a flat 25% assumption in a yellow cell.
- Blue text is typed-in input, black text is a formula, green text pulls from another tab.

## Tools

Microsoft Excel (formulas, cross-sheet references, number formatting).

## What I learned

- Sales grew 15% and profit margin went up from 8.3% to 9.8%.
- The company owes less compared to what it owns (debt-to-equity went from 0.83 to 0.71).
- The income statement and balance sheet are connected, and the balance check should always be zero.
